# Acme Expansion Goes Live
Date: 2026-09-04
Author: customer-success

Acme moved from pilot to their full contract today. Their whole
document backlog is now flowing through the platform.

What changed on our side:

- Acme request volume is expected to roughly triple.
- Their workload skews heavily toward document_extract. Their
  contracts and filings run much longer than our median document,
  commonly 40 to 300 pages.
- No infrastructure changes were made for this. We are absorbing the
  volume on existing capacity.

Flagging for the platform team since Acme will now be a visible share
of aggregate metrics.
