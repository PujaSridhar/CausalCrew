# Shipping Fee Update — West Region
Date: 2026-08-27
Author: pricing
PR: #3391

Following the renegotiation of our West region carrier contract, the flat
shipping fee for West orders rises from $4.99 to $8.99 effective today.

- Applies to all orders shipped to the West region.
- East, South, and North regions are unchanged at $4.99.
- The fee is shown at checkout, after the cart is built.

We did not run a staged test. Carrier costs made the change time-sensitive.
Pricing will review order volume after two weeks.
